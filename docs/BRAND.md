# CurioDrop — Brand kit

## Name & logo

- **Wordmark:** `CurioDrop` (Inter Bold, letter-spacing -0.02em)
- **Symbol:** a soft-blue water drop with a subtle brain-fold pattern inside
- **Icon file:** generate via prompt →
  `"minimal flat vector logo, water droplet shape, gentle brain gyri texture inside, single soft blue #5B8CFF gradient, white background, no text, centered, 1024x1024"`
  (Midjourney, Ideogram, or fal.ai Flux)

## Colors

| Role       | Hex       | Use                                |
| ---------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Primary    | `#5B8CFF` | logo, caption highlights           |
| Accent     | `#FFD166` | hook chip, "Did you know?" badge   |
| Ink        | `#0F172A` | dark text on light bg              |
| Paper      | `#F8FAFC` | light bg                           |

## Typography

- **Titles / captions:** Inter Bold (already installed via Dockerfile).
- **Kicker chip:** Inter ExtraBold, all-caps, letter-spacing 0.08em.

## Voice & tone

- Warm, playful, kid-safe. Wonder over shock.
- Never uses "insane", "shocking", "you won't believe". Uses "tiny", "quietly", "actually".
- Ends every video with: **"Follow for a new mind-drop every day."**

## Video look

- Cinematic realism (Kling / Flux) with soft golden hour lighting.
- 3-second "hook chip" in the top-third for the first 2 seconds: yellow #FFD166 background, "Did you know?" in ink.
- Captions in the bottom third, 2-5 words per chunk, white with 40 %-opacity dark outline.
- 3-second brand outro fades in the CurioDrop wordmark + water-drop symbol.
